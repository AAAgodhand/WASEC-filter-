How to read the columns:  ID = our internal reference number (matches the Excel workbook).  Status = what kind of attention the lead needs — Verified means we checked the live page ourselves; the others are the filter's assessment and still need a human check.  Finding = what we know and what to do next.  Possible member fits = an automatic first guess only — always confirm a member's real capability before contacting them.  The grey pill under each agency name is the industry of the work.
On "social impact" in contracts — an honest finding. You asked which of these contracts are looking for social impact. Across all 687 opportunities, not one carried an explicit social-procurement clause (no mention of social enterprise, supplier diversity, or buy-social requirements). Fifteen contain community or wellbeing wording, but that wording comes from the service category itself (e.g. "Community and social services") rather than from a buyer asking for social impact — so we have not marked them as social-impact opportunities. This absence is itself useful intelligence: WA's social procurement framework is not yet showing up in the tender documents themselves.

How this list was made: an automated filter removed expired, duplicate, overseas/interstate, research-programme and equipment-only tenders from the 687 collected records, then we personally checked the shortlist against the live tender pages. Those checks changed 10 of 16 machine assessments — catching things no automatic filter can see, such as compulsory briefings that had already been held and credential requirements. That is why every unverified lead should be opened and checked before it goes to a member.
